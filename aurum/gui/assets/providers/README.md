# Connection button artwork

The Redbark symbol belongs to Redbark. The local PNG uses the red symbol from the official dark artwork.
Aurum displays its silhouette in white at 14px. The black backing and surrounding margin were removed so the button can supply its own hover and focus fills.
[Redbark brand kit](https://redbark.com/brand) · [Original symbol](https://redbark.com/images/redbark-symbol-dark.png)

The white Blossom belongs to OpenAI. The bundled SVG is the original `OpenAI-white-monoblossom.svg` from the
official logo pack. Aurum uses it to identify the ChatGPT connection.
[OpenAI brand guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/) · [Original logo pack](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip)
